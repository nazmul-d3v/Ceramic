Unwavering Dedication to Excellence & Innovation

Since its establishment, Heeds Ceramics Industries Ltd. has emerged as the premier leader in the Bangladesh tiles market. Spanning a massive 67-acre state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in Mukkhopur, Trishal, Mymensingh, we produce 25.55 million square meters of high-density ceramic and vitrified tiles annually.

Sophisticated Designs

Sustainable Manufacturing

EUROPEAN PRECISION & GREEN EARTH

World-Class Tech & Eco-Sustainability

SACMI Continua+ Line

Imported Italian continuous compaction technology yielding maximum density, zero micro-cracks, and high breaking strength across all vitrified tiles.

HD Inkjet Digital Printing

8-color ultra HD digital inkjet matrix capturing natural marble veins, stone textures, and wooden grains with 100% natural color accuracy.

Zero Water Waste System

100% wastewater treatment plant (ETP) at Trishal facility recycling every drop back into production, setting green industry standards in Bangladesh.

Diamond Glaze Coating

Scratch-resistant micro-crystal glaze technology protecting surface shine against heavy foot traffic, acid spills, and UV fading.
